# Application quality checklist

## Decision integrity

- Check duplicates before producing new work.
- State the recruitment market and hard-eligibility conclusion first.
- Separate mandatory requirements from preferences.
- Report an evidence-based overall match score out of 10 when evaluating candidate-role fit.
- Do not let a fit score conceal a material eligibility failure.

## Evidence integrity

- Trace every substantive claim to a resume, project artifact, dataset, code file, report, or verified user statement.
- Label unsupported JD requirements as gaps instead of implying experience.
- Audit every experience bullet for owned action, context, evidence, and outcome or milestone.
- Preserve useful verified metrics with units and business context.
- Do not invent numbers, tools, motivation, credentials, or employment.

## Resume quality

- Use a role-specific one-line summary.
- Keep employment and project experience separate.
- Put the strongest role-relevant evidence first.
- Use ATS terminology naturally and only when accurate.
- Meet the requested page limit without tiny type, altered margins, or weak filler.
- Preserve the approved template's visual identity unless redesign was requested.

## Cover-letter quality

- Reach role-specific motivation quickly.
- Add interpretation and motivation rather than repeating resume chronology.
- Include at least one distinctive, evidence-grounded statement.
- Use plain professional language and a restrained close.

## File and render quality

- Keep one canonical DOCX and one PDF per application.
- Export PDF from the final approved DOCX only.
- Regenerate the PDF after every DOCX change.
- Inspect every rendered page for overflow, clipping, overlap, broken bullets, blank pages, alignment, spacing, and font consistency.
- Confirm matching content, order, pagination, and line breaks across DOCX and PDF.
- Preserve unrelated applications, historical files, tracker rows, formulas, and validations.
- During an end-to-end application, update the tracker without requiring a separate request.
- Populate supported existing fields such as date, company, role, status, next action, referrer, job ID, and URL from the JD, job page, context, application files, and system date.
- Leave unsupported tracker values blank rather than inventing or requesting non-blocking information.

## Handoff quality

- State the recommendation or conclusion first.
- Link final deliverables.
- Name unsupported requirements that were intentionally omitted.
- Report file and tracker changes.
- For substantive revisions, provide `Added`, `Removed`, and `Rewritten` summaries.
